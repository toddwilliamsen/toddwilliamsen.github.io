Look n Cook

A cooking assistant for families—photograph what you have, confirm ingredients, and get recipe matches. Photos and matching run on the phone by default.

Other projects·Not cloud security

This one sits outside the security lane on purpose. Look n Cook is a consumer mobile app: point the camera at what’s in the fridge or pantry, confirm what the on-device model sees, and match against a large offline recipe library.

What it is

  • Expo / React Native app (iOS primary)
  • On-device vision via ClearSight AI (Liquid LFM2.5-VL through react-native-executorch)
  • Offline recipe library—thousands of real recipes, plus curated wild-game recipes

What it is not

It is not Azure, Sentinel, Zero Trust, ARM, or Quantum Helix. Those live under Security & cloud.
